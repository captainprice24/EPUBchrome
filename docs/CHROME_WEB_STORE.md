# Chrome Web Store submission

Everything needed to publish Folio in the Chrome Web Store: how to build the upload, ready-to-paste listing text, the answers for the **Privacy** tab, and a final checklist.

## 1. Build the upload

```sh
npm ci
npm run package        # writes .output/folio-<version>-chrome.zip
```

Upload that ZIP; it has `manifest.json` at its root, as the store requires.

**Build it without a Google OAuth client.** Leave `WXT_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` empty in `.env`. The store build then requests a single permission (`unlimitedStorage`), makes no network requests and hides **Open from Drive**. Drive import uses the `drive.readonly` scope, which Google classifies as restricted: publishing it requires OAuth verification and a security assessment. Add Drive in a later release, preferably with the `drive.file` scope and the Google Picker.

Check the generated manifest before uploading:

```sh
unzip -p .output/folio-*-chrome.zip manifest.json
```

It should list `"permissions": ["unlimitedStorage"]`, the four icons, `minimum_chrome_version` `147`, and no `oauth2` or `host_permissions`.

### Detailed description

> Folio is a private reading room for your EPUB books, right in Chrome. Import a book from your computer and it stays in your browser: no account, no registration, no ads and no fees.
>
> **Read comfortably**
> • Turn pages horizontally or read in a vertical scroll
> • Choose the publisher's typeface or one of three reading fonts, and adjust text size, line spacing and margins
> • Paper, Sepia and Night page themes
> • Fullscreen reading that keeps your contents, bookmarks and notes within reach
> • Folio remembers your place, even after you change the text size or window
>
> **Keep what matters**
> • Bookmarks and highlights that stay attached to the right passage
> • Search inside the chapter or page you are reading
> • Export your notes, or download a full backup of your library and restore it later
>
> **More than EPUB**
> • DRM-free MOBI and KF8 books
> • PDF and DjVu documents shown as their original pages, with zoom, text selection and outlines
> • Markdown files
> • Print an EPUB chapter or a whole book with Chrome's "Save as PDF", or download the untouched original of any PDF
>
> **Private by design**
> Your books, notes and reading positions are stored only in this browser on your device. Folio has no servers, no analytics and no tracking, and it blocks web content embedded in books, so opening a book never contacts anyone.
>
> Folio does not open DRM-protected books or password-protected PDFs. Clearing browser data or uninstalling Folio removes the local library, so keep your original files and use Storage & backup.

## 3. Privacy tab answers

**Single purpose:**

> Folio lets you read and organise your own e-books and documents (EPUB, MOBI, PDF, DjVu and Markdown) in a private local library in the browser.

**Permission justification, `unlimitedStorage`:**

> Folio stores the user's imported books and documents, and their bookmarks and highlights, locally in IndexedDB. E-book libraries can exceed the default storage quota. Unlimited storage keeps the library from being evicted by the browser. No data leaves the device.

**Remote code:** No, I am not using remote code. All scripts, workers, WebAssembly and fonts are packaged in the extension. The content security policy is `script-src 'self' 'wasm-unsafe-eval'`. `wasm-unsafe-eval` is needed for the bundled DjVu decoder and the PDF image and colour decoders.

**Data usage:** tick **none** of the data categories. Folio does not collect or transmit any user data.

Certify all three statements:

- I do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, outside of the approved use cases.
- I do not use or transfer user data for purposes that are unrelated to my item's single purpose.
- I do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

**Privacy policy URL:** https://captainprice24.github.io/EPUBchrome/privacy.html. It is published with the GitHub Pages site from `web/privacy.html`.

## 5. Checklist

- [ ] Chrome Web Store developer account registered (one-time registration fee) with 2-Step Verification on the Google account.
- [ ] Publisher contact email verified in the developer dashboard.
- [ ] Trader or non-trader status declared for EU customers in the dashboard.
- [ ] The GitHub Pages deployment has published `privacy.html`, and the privacy policy URL opens.
- [ ] `npm run package` built without `WXT_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, and the manifest is checked as in §1.
- [ ] The ZIP was loaded unpacked in a clean Chrome profile: the toolbar icon opens Folio; a second click focuses the same tab; the sample book, an EPUB, a PDF, highlights and backup all work.
- [ ] Listing text, graphics and Privacy tab filled in from §2 and §3.
- [ ] After approval, point the web preview's **Get Chrome extension** link and the README at the store listing instead of the ZIP download.

## Notes for reviewers (optional field)

> Folio is a local e-book reader. To try it, click the toolbar icon, then choose "Open the sample" in the library, or import any DRM-free EPUB or PDF. The extension requests only unlimitedStorage and makes no network requests.
